1 PROBLEM STATEMENT
Modern data-driven applications require users to explore and interpret increasingly large and complex datasets. However, many
existing database systems assume that users possess technical expertise in querying languages such as SQL, which creates a significant
barrier for non-technical users. As a result, users often struggle to
extract meaningful insights from data, limiting the accessibility and
usability of data-driven tools.
Human-centered data exploration aims to address this challenge
by designing systems that enable intuitive, interactive, and accessible ways for users to understand data. Prior research has explored
various approaches, including visual analytics and visualization recommendation systems such as Voyager [4] and SeeDB [3], natural
language interfaces for querying databases such as Seq2SQL [5],
and intelligent data exploration and visualization systems such as
DeepEye [2]. These systems aim to reduce cognitive load, guide
users toward relevant insights, and support exploratory workflows.
Despite these advances, there remains a gap in lightweight, userfriendly tools that combine interactivity, automation, and domainspecific exploration. In particular, users exploring domain-specific
datasets (such as books, movies, or personal collections) often lack
tools that provide both structured querying and intuitive exploration
capabilities.
In this project, we propose an Interactive Book Data Exploration Assistant, a human-centered system that enables users to explore a book dataset through visualizations, summaries, and guided
interactions. The system is designed to help users answer questions
such as identifying popular genres, discovering trends, and understanding dataset characteristics without requiring technical expertise.
This problem is important because improving data accessibility
can empower a broader range of users, including students, casual
users, and domain enthusiasts, to make informed decisions based on
data. By lowering the barrier to data exploration, such systems can
significantly enhance usability and user engagement.
Our approach is inspired by prior work in interactive data exploration and visualization recommendation [3, 4], data transformation
and cleaning systems such as Data Wrangler [1], and AI-assisted
analytics systems such as DeepEye [2], and aims to demonstrate how
these ideas can be applied in a practical, domain-specific setting.
